Good questions

Answers before you register.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to do this?

No. If you can use email and a web browser, you can do this. We build live with you, one step at a time, and every session is recorded so you can go back over anything. Plenty of leaders come in unsure about the tech and leave running their own agents.

What will I need to participate?

A Windows or Mac laptop or desktop you can install software on, with at a minimum 16 GB of RAM and 500 GB of storage. We recommend 24 GB of RAM and 1 TB. This runs real work on your own machine, so a phone, tablet, or Chromebook will not do it, and it is not a place to skimp. You will also need a reliable internet connection and at least a Claude Pro subscription, about $20 a month, to build, run, and manage agents. We will tell you exactly what to set up before Day 1.

Can I register my whole team at once?

Yes, and most people do. Choose "My company or organization" on the form and list each person's name and email. If you do not have the list yet, register your organization now and send us the names later. We just need them by Wednesday, October 14, so everyone is set up before Day 1. You receive one invoice for the group.

Are there any additional costs?

The $1,000 per person covers the full week of training. The only other cost is a Claude Pro subscription for each person, about $20 a month. That is it, no hidden fees.

Is there anything I need to do before Day 1?

Yes. There is a short round of prework to get you fully set up: your computer ready, Cowork by Claude installed, and your Claude Pro subscription active. Once we begin there is no time to stop and fix tech issues, so our team makes sure every person is prepped and ready before Monday, October 19. That is why registration closes Wednesday, October 14.

Are the sessions online or in person?

Everything is virtual. All sessions are live on Zoom at 10:00 AM Central, and your Zoom links are posted in Basecamp.

What if I have to miss a day?

You will be fine. Every session is recorded, and the recording and transcript are posted in Basecamp for you to keep. All of your prompts, homework, and assignments live there too, so if you miss a morning you can watch the recording, grab everything you need, and be caught up for the next day.

Will there be time for questions?

Yes. Each day I teach for an hour and then dismiss the group, but I stick around for a few minutes afterward for a quick Q&A. If something was not clear, stay on and ask.

How is the Sprint run day to day?

We run the whole project through Basecamp. Your prompts, homework, session recordings, and where you submit your work all live there. You receive your Basecamp invitation once you register and pay.

Can I share the agents, prompts, and training videos with others in my organization?

Please do not. These systems and tools are our intellectual property, and we have invested heavily in building them for you. If you know someone who would benefit, I would be grateful if you pointed them to us, or brought them through the Sprint with you, so they get the full program for themselves. You are on the honor system here, and I trust you. I am proving it by handing you everything.

What if my organization will not let me use Claude?

That is a question specific to your situation, so let's have a quick side conversation to find the best solution. I am also glad to speak with your IT team about why these particular tools are needed. For building agents and running them together as a coordinated system, Cowork by Claude is, in our experience, the strongest tool for this work today, and it is what this entire Sprint is built around.
